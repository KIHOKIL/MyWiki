Title: TSMC Widens Foundry Lead as AI Demand Soars

Published: Wed, 16 Sep 2026 05:40:00 GMT

Although the global foundry market grew to its largest size in the second quarter driven by demand for artificial intelligence (AI) semiconductors, the market share gap between TSMC and Samsung Electronics was found to have widened further. While TSMC strengthens its dominance in advanced processes centered on 3-nanometer and 5-nanometer, Samsung Electronics sets out to recover its performance in the second half of the year, spearheaded by the full operation of its 4-nanometer lines, price increases, and yield improvements in the 2-nanometer process. According to the industry on Sept. 16, TSMC’s consolidated revenue in August was 514.81 billion Taiwan dollars (about $16.3 billion), an increase of 53.3% compared to the same period last year. It increased by 10.1% compared to July, breaking the all-time monthly record. This is the effect of expanded orders for advanced processes as demand for AI accelerators and big tech companies’ self-designed semiconductors increased. The entire global foundry market is also growing rapidly, riding on AI demand. According to the market research firm TrendForce, the combined revenue of the top 10 global foundry companies in the second quarter of this year reached a record high of $53.49 billion, an 11.5% increase compared to the previous quarter. However, the market growth trend shows a pattern of concentration on TSMC. TSMC’s market share rose by 0.2%p from 72.3% in the first quarter to 72.5% in the second quarter, firmly securing its first place. Samsung Foundry maintained its second place, but its market share dropped by 0.6%p from 6.5% to 5.9% during the same period. Accordingly, the market share gap between the first and second place, TSMC and Samsung Electronics, expanded by 0.8%p compared to the previous quarter. TSMC is expanding its revenue from advanced processes.
