Title: HP Printing Korea, Global Technology Form Printing Systems Partnership
Link: http://www.bing.com/news/apiclick.aspx?ref=FexRss&aid=&tid=6aa94d13176445d78a32bd8e46b6b807&url=https%3a%2f%2fwww.thelec.net%2fnews%2farticleView.html%3fidxno%3d13724&c=5305720528909893338&mkt=ko-kr
Published: Tue, 08 Sep 2026 04:31:00 GMT

HP Printing Korea said on Sept. 7 that it has selected semiconductor design company Global Technology as its hardware engineering technical support partner for printing systems and signed a strategic partnership agreement. The companies plan to jointly advance electronic component and circuit development as well as artificial intelligence (AI) technologies while strengthening their technological competitiveness. The two companies will collaborate on the design and development of electronic components and circuits and jointly develop AI-based technologies. Global Technology will also take full responsibility for hardware engineering technical support for HP Printing Korea’s research and development organization. Global Technology will provide technical support throughout the product development process, including design changes and prototyping for printed circuit assemblies (PCAs). The company said it will operate an integrated technical support system based on its own engineering capabilities and a network of proven Korean partners. The companies plan to gradually expand the scope of their partnership and jointly develop next-generation main controllers for printing systems, leveraging Global Technology’s system semiconductor and application design capabilities.
