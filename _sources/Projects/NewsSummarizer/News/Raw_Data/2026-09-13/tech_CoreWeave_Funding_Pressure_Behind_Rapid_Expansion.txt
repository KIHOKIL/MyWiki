The revenue multiple reveals only half the truth.CRWV's "cheapness" is built entirely on its aggressive debt financing and capital structure. CoreWeave's core business model is effectively monetizing long-term customer contracts up front by pledging them, using extreme financial leverage to fuel capital expenditures and compute construction. However, after accounting for heavy hardware depreciation, the company's current operating profit falls severely short of covering interest expenses, while free cash flow continues to suffer massive cash burn. We believe CoreWeave's valuation discount relative to Nebius is justified to some extent, reflecting its high leverage, customer concentration, and uncertainty over GPU asset value. Until post-depreciation profitability, interest coverage, and cash flow deficits show sustained improvement, we maintain a bearish stance on CRWV.
