You're looking at $30 billion in OpenAI alone, a $12.9 billion Hugging Face acquisition, and stakes in nearly every major AI company on the planet — here's the full map. Table of Content Most Read Sponsored TL;DR There’s a number buried in NVIDIA’s latest financial disclosures that tells you more about the company’s future than any earnings call ever could: $99 billion. That’s the current value of NVIDIA’s equity investments in AI companies — up from $7 billion just twelve months ago, and from $2.2 billion two years before that. This isn’t a chip company anymore. It’s becoming something the tech industry hasn’t really seen before. To understand the scale of whatNVIDIA has done, consider this: the company committed over $40 billion to new financing deals in 2026 alone. CFO Colette Kress noted that nearly $50 billion of the total portfolio is directed at frontier AI labs — the companies building the most advanced AI models.  That kind of growth doesn’t happen through patient, diversified investing. It happens when a company decides that its biggest customers are also its biggest bets. Here’s where the money went: NVIDIA is investing across the entire AI value chain. Foundation model companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI). Infrastructure providers (CoreWeave, Nebius). Optical and networking hardware (Corning, Marvell, Lumentum, Coherent). Developer tools (Hugging Face). Even code-generation startups (Poolside). On the surface, NVIDIA investing in its biggest customers looks like a straightforward loyalty play: fund the companies that buy your GPUs, and they’ll keep buying your GPUs. But there’s something more strategic happening. NVIDIA isn’t just buying equity — it’s weaving itself into the financial structure of the AI industry in a way that creates deep dependencies.
