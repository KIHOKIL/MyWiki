Title: Hotter-Than-Expected US CPI Leads Wall Street to Raise August Core PCE Forecasts

Published: Fri, 11 Sep 2026 22:50:00 GMT

Summary Forecast Trend Report by Period Major Wall Street banks have raised their forecasts for the August core personal consumption expenditures price index after a stronger-than-expected US consumer price index report. Walter Bloomberg reported on September 12 that Barclays lifted its forecast for August core PCE on a month-over-month basis to 0.25% from 0.22%, while Goldman Sachs raised its estimate to 0.26% from 0.24%. Bank of America increased its forecast to 0.30% from 0.26%, TD raised its call to 0.32% from 0.24%, and Nomura lifted its estimate to 0.278% from 0.245%. Mizuho projected a 0.30% increase under its previous calculation method and as much as 0.40% under a new methodology.
