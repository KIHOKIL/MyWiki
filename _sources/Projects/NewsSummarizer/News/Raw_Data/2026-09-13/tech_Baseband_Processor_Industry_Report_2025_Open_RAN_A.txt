How Is 5G Revolutionizing the Capabilities and Design of Baseband Processors?The transition to 5G technology is dramatically reshaping the architecture, capabilities, and competitive landscape of the global baseband processor market. Unlike previous generations, 5G demands a significant leap in data processing power, energy efficiency, and integration of multi-band, multi-mode compatibility to support diverse use cases ranging from enhanced mobile broadband to ultra-reliable low-latency communication (URLLC). Baseband processors must now handle vastly more data, orchestrate massive MIMO configurations, and support millimeter-wave (mmWave) and sub-6 GHz frequencies each of which presents unique design and thermal challenges.
